Link Lite Recorder

Buying and the licence key

EUR 1.99, once. No subscription, no account. Here is exactly what happens when you buy.

  1. 01

    Press Buy in the extension

    The payment page is run by Lemon Squeezy, which sells Link and handles the payment and tax. The price shown includes tax.

    Buy · EUR 1.99
  2. 02

    You receive a licence key by email

    It arrives at the address you gave Lemon Squeezy, usually within a few minutes. Check spam if it is not there.

    XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXXYOUR KEY
  3. 03

    Paste the key into Link's panel once

    Open the panel on any stream page, choose Enter key, paste. It can be active in up to three browsers.

    KEY ACCEPTED
Trial

What the trial is

+

Link Lite works in full for the first recordings, with no key and no account. After that it asks for a key before recording again. Nothing else changes.

Browsers

Moving a key to another browser

+

Paste the same key in the new browser. One key can be active in up to three browsers. To free a place, choose Remove key in the browser you no longer use, or write to support.

Refund

The 14-day money-back promise

+

If Link Lite does not do what you need, write within 14 days of buying, say what went wrong, and the payment is returned. There is no form to fill in.

Tax

The price includes tax

+

EUR 1.99 is the whole amount. Lemon Squeezy works out and pays any tax for your country, so nothing is added at checkout.

Account

There is no account

+

The key is the only thing to keep. Save the email, or ask support to send it again to the address you bought with.

Contact

Who to write to

For keys, refunds and anything else: support@linkturbo.app. Or ask in Discord.
