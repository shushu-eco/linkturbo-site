LINK TURBO

A standalone program for Windows. It records up to 3 Kick or Twitch streams at once, finds the moments for you, edits finished 9:16 clips, and posts them. And with Link Night it goes on collecting while you sleep. For streams you have the right to use.

Five steps

From live stream to posted clip, on one computer.

  1. RECORD01 Studio Up to 3 Kick or Twitch streams at once, on your own computer.
  2. FIND02 Moments found for you Up to 30 ranked moments per recording, with the time they happened.
  3. EDIT03 Finished 9:16 clips Title, hook and captions timed to the voice.
  4. POST04 YouTube Shorts and Instagram Post the clips you choose. Only those leave your computer.
  5. MEASURE05 Analytics See how each posted clip did: over 7 days, 28 days or all time.
Link Night · part of Link TURBO

It records while you sleep.

Choose the hours, press Start night, and go. Link Night records your streams three at a time, checks every recording, and has the moments ranked for you when you are back.

Made for your own material: your own channel and the streams you have the right to use. Night only collects. Nothing is cut into a clip or posted until you have looked at it yourself.

HOURS

You choose how long

From 1 to 12 hours. Press Start night and go. It ends by itself.

THREE AT ONCE

Three streams side by side

Recorded in 30-minute stretches, one after the other, for as long as the night lasts.

YOUR MATERIAL

Made for your own streams

Your own channel, and the channels you have permission to clip. It works through the night on material that is yours to use.

CHECKED

Every recording is checked

Whole length, sound, and the moments found, before the next stretch begins. A stream that goes offline is replaced.

REMOTE

Watch it from your phone

Updates in your own Discord server: progress, a picture of the night, and a warning at once if something stops. Start, stop or continue from there.

CAREFUL

Looks after the computer

Keeps it awake while it works, says beforehand how much room the night will take, stops before the disk fills, and can put it to sleep when done.

Nothing is uploaded except the clips you choose to post. Recordings, moments and edits stay on your Windows machine. Link TURBO is for streams you have the right to use.
