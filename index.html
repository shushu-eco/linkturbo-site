What it does

One recording, with the moments already marked.

RECORD

Records the stream

Picture and sound, cut out of the page. One mp4, up to 30 minutes.

MARK

Marks highlights

A hotkey and a few words. Or by itself, when chat speeds up.

REWIND

Rewind

Switched on, it keeps the last minutes, so a moment can be saved after it happened.

EXPORT

For your editor

The marks come with the recording: a list to copy, or a marker file for DaVinci Resolve.

LOCAL

Stays on your computer

Nothing is uploaded. No analytics. Chat is only counted: never who wrote, or what.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. 1
    Open a streamOn Kick or Twitch, in your own browser tab. Press the Link button and the panel opens on the page.
  2. 2
    Press RecordMark a highlight with a hotkey whenever something happens. Stop when you are done.
  3. 3
    Get one file with your marksAn mp4 in your Downloads folder, and the list of marks ready for your editor.

Questions

Asked most often.

+Does it work on any stream?

It works on Kick and Twitch stream pages in Chrome, Edge or Brave. Use it for streams you have the right to record.

+Where does the file go?

Into a folder named Link in your browser’s Downloads folder, as one mp4 named after the channel, the date and the time. Nothing leaves your computer.

+How long can one recording be?

Up to 30 minutes. Start again for a new file.

+What happens after the trial?

The first recordings work in full. After that, Link asks for a key. The key costs EUR 1.99, once, and works in up to three browsers.

+Is there a refund?

Yes. Write to support@linkturbo.app within 14 days of buying.
