Why did you remove it?

One question, so the next version is better. Answer it or close this page: nothing is sent unless you press Send.

The main reason
Goes by e-mail to the one person who makes Link.

What Send passes on: the reason you chose, what you wrote, the extension's version, how many days it was installed and whether it ever saved a recording. Nothing else, and no name. Form.taxi, the service that passes it on (in the EU), also sees your IP address, as any website you visit does. Want an answer? Write to support@linkturbo.app. Changed your mind? Add it again.
